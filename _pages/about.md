I am a 5th year PhD candidate in the Department of Geophysical Sciences at the University of Chicago. My research focuses on modeling the evolving chemistry and dynamics of protoplanetary disks, the birthplaces of planetary systems. I am interested in understanding how dynamic processes can influence the chemistry taking place in disks, and how they can set the spatial distributions of molecular species, both in solids and in the gas. I am also interested in understanding how present-day observations of small, icy bodies like comets can help us understand the conditions of the protoplanetary disk at the time of planet formation.
